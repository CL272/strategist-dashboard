Source: Yahoo Finance (indices, ETFs, FX, commodities, crypto, VIX/MOVE); FRED, US Treasury, MOF Japan, ECB, SingStat/MAS, Bank Negara (yields, spreads). Hover a row for its exact series.

Correlations use returns: % change for prices; for yields and spreads, the bp change with its sign flipped by default so a fall in yield counts as a gain, as it is for a bondholder (switch with “Yields as”). Positive stocks vs yields then means stocks and bonds move together. Each figure covers the window ending on the “To” date. Asian and US markets close at different times, so daily figures understate their link. Use Weekly for pairs across regions.

Public data only: FRED (St. Louis Fed), US Treasury, MOF Japan, ECB, SingStat/MAS, DOSM, Bank Negara, OECD, Bank of Japan, e-Stat, OpenRouter, Epoch AI, Robert Shiller (Yale), NY Fed, CFTC, Yahoo Finance and public news RSS feeds, refreshed every weekday. For research use; not investment advice.
